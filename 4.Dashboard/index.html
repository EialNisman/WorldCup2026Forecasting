Probability adjustments

Why the forecast caps some extreme probabilities

This World Cup increased participating countries by 50%. The model was trained only on previous World Cup data, so it treats some 2026 teams as extreme outliers. There have not been World Cup matchups with this much difference in team metrics, which can lead the model to give extremely high win probabilities to superior teams playing against much lower-rated teams.

The following arbitrary adjustments were made to take this into account:

  • Group-stage winning odds are capped at 94%. This was decided based on the maximum odds a team has ever had to win a World Cup group-stage game, according to bookmakers.
  • Knockout stages are capped at 80% odds of winning in two-way matchups with no ties. This was also based on bookmaker odds, as only one game has ever shown odds above 80% for a team to win a knockout-stage game.

The point is to give more probability mass to upsets, which are real possibilities in football. This is a domain adjustment: a model trained to predict games usually between top-50 ranked nations is not particularly equipped to predict games involving much lower-rated teams.

For context, between the 2010 and 2022 World Cups, an average of 3 teams per tournament were ranked 50 or below. For the 2026 World Cup, 12 teams are ranked 50 or below.
